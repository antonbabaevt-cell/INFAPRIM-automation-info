Usage information

10 November 2026

INFAPRIM GitHub Automation is a code-delivery tool operated by Anton Babaev for his calendar project. OAuth authorization is intended for the owner and authorized maintainers. Calendar visitors do not need to authorize this client.

The initial target is a separate TEST script. Production delivery requires explicit owner approval and a recorded rollback version. This information page does not approve a release, provide a paid service, or guarantee uninterrupted availability.

Use only projects and credentials you are authorized to manage. Do not post secrets or private records in the public repository. The operator can revoke Google access and disable workflows at any time.
